3 Grid & conversion ChiselGraph: 1 block = 16×16 voxels

Dither strength 35%
Lower strength keeps large color areas cleaner. At 0%, every mode behaves like direct palette matching.
Smoothing (anti-alias) 50%
0% = hard pixel edges (nearest-neighbor, crisp retro look) · 100% = maximum smoothing (soft anti-aliased edges, best for photos). The blur scales with how far the crop is downscaled to the grid.
Export size: 32×16 px — exact multiples of 16 so ChiselGraph maps 1 px = 1 voxel per block face.
