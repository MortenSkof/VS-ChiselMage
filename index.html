3 Grid & conversion ChiselGraph: 1 block = 16×16 voxels

—
Emulator screenshots and 2×/4× ports are integer upscales. Recovering the native grid first keeps every sprite pixel intact instead of resampling it into mush. Leave on auto for scans and photos.
Dither strength 25%
Lower strength keeps large color areas cleaner. At 0%, every mode behaves like direct palette matching.
Edge cut-off 40%
A voxel turns solid only when at least this share of it covers the subject. Raise it to thin the silhouette, lower it to keep more outline detail.
Export size: 32×16 px — exact multiples of 16 so ChiselGraph maps 1 px = 1 voxel per block face.
